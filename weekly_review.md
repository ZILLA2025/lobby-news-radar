**What separated them**

- **Length:** 16–21 s campaign Reels did fine. The 24–30 s ones held people longer per view but skipped more. Keep
  12–20 s.

## 3. Three changes for next week (each written as a test)

1. **Opening (this week's A/B test):** *If our own news Reels open on a moving official trailer shot with the
   headline already on screen (no title card, no logo), skip rate drops below 55% (now 77%).* A = current style,
   B = action-first. Run on similar news topics; nothing else changes.
2. **Topic:** *If every news Reel leads with one specific new thing (a named location, weapon, map or GTA VI
   feature) instead of a date or a round-up, we average 2+ shares per Reel (now 0.4).*
3. **Giveaway:** *If the Ghost wallpaper giveaway Reel opens on the best wallpaper in motion with "Comment GHOST"
   on screen from second 1, it gets 10+ comments and 10+ new followers (last time: 2 comments, 87 views).*
